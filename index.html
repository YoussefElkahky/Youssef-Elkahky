About

I’m a cybersecurity and IT professional building my career in penetration testing, with hands-on experience in Linux, networking, web security, and security labs.

I’m now looking for my first professional opportunity in the technical field: penetration testing, cybersecurity, vulnerability assessment, IT support, and Linux/network support.
